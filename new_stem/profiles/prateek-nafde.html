Roles & Responsibilities

  • Gather, analyze, and interpret supply chain data to identify trends and operational insights.
  • Develop and maintain supply chain dashboards, reports, and performance visualizations for stakeholders.
  • Collect, clean, and validate supply chain data to ensure accuracy and consistency.
  • Perform supply chain analysis to support planning, forecasting, and business decision-making.
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams to understand supply chain requirements and objectives.
  • Identify process improvement opportunities through data-driven analysis and recommendations.
  • Monitor key performance indicators (KPIs) and track supply chain performance metrics.
  • Conduct ad hoc analysis to address supply chain challenges and support operational needs.
  • Create and maintain documentation for supply chain models, reports, and analytical processes.
  • Ensure data integrity, governance, and compliance with supply chain standards.
  • Present supply chain findings and actionable recommendations to management and stakeholders.
